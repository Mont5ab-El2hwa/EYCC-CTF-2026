# Kazyon

## Description

Mo Bakr took this photo while standing outside a branch of one of Egypt's most recognizable supermarket chains, but he forgot to share the location.

Your mission is to analyze the photo and identify the exact branch:
1. The branch’s full address.
2. The branch’s telephone number.

> **Example:** `EYCC{27_Mahmoud_Nader_Fawzy_St_Maadii_01123456789}`
